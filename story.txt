The Little Village

Once upon a time there was a small village near a green forest. The village was quiet and peaceful. Small houses stood beside a narrow road, and colorful flowers grew near every door.

In the village lived a young boy named Arun. Arun was a curious boy who loved to explore new places. Every morning he walked around the village and watched the birds flying in the sky. He often wondered what was hidden inside the large forest.

One sunny morning Arun decided to visit the forest. He packed some bread, a bottle of water, and a small bag. Then he told his mother that he would return before sunset.

Arun walked along the narrow road and soon reached the forest. The trees were tall and green. Birds were singing in the branches, and a cool wind moved through the leaves.

Arun walked deeper into the forest. After some time he found a small wooden house. The house looked very old, but there was smoke coming from the chimney.

Arun knocked on the door. An old man opened the door and smiled at him.

The old man invited Arun inside the house. There was a small table near the window and a warm fire in the corner. The old man gave Arun some fresh fruit and asked him why he had come to the forest.

Arun told the old man that he wanted to explore the forest. The old man smiled and said that the forest was full of interesting places, but Arun should always be careful.

The old man then showed Arun a small map. The map had a river, a hill, and a beautiful lake marked on it.

Arun was excited when he saw the map. He thanked the old man and followed the path shown on the map.

After walking for a long time Arun reached a small river. The water was clear and clean. Small fish were swimming in the river.

Arun sat beside the river and ate his bread. He watched the fish moving through the water. After resting for a while he continued his journey.

Soon Arun reached a green hill. From the top of the hill he could see the entire forest. He could also see his village far away.

Arun felt happy because he had discovered a beautiful place. He wanted to stay longer, but the sun was slowly going down.

Arun remembered his promise to his mother. He quickly walked back toward the wooden house.

When Arun reached the house, the old man was waiting outside. The old man told Arun that it was time to return home.

Arun thanked the old man for his kindness. The old man gave Arun a small wooden box and told him to open it when he reached home.

Arun started walking back to the village. The forest looked different in the evening. The sky was orange and the birds were returning to their nests.

After some time Arun reached his village. His mother was waiting near the door.

His mother was happy to see him. Arun told her about the forest, the old man, the river, and the green hill.

Then Arun opened the small wooden box. Inside the box was a beautiful blue stone.

Arun asked his mother what the stone could be. His mother looked at the stone and smiled.

She told Arun that the stone was a reminder of his journey. She said that the most valuable thing was not the stone, but the memories he had made.

Arun kept the blue stone safely in his room. From that day he became even more curious about the world.

Every morning Arun looked at the blue stone and remembered the old man.

He learned that every journey could teach something new. He also learned that curiosity was useful, but being careful was important too.

Arun continued to explore new places with his friends. Whenever they went somewhere new, they remembered to stay together and help each other.

The small village remained peaceful, but Arun's world became much bigger.

He discovered that the world was full of stories, people, and beautiful places waiting to be explored.
